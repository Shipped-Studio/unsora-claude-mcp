# ClipCast

ClipCast turns long videos, podcasts and streams into short clips with AI, then
posts or schedules those clips to your connected social accounts: YouTube,
TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Threads, Bluesky, X and
Google Business Profile.

## What it does

- **Clipping:** give Claude a video link (or upload a file) and ClipCast finds
  the best moments, cuts them into vertical or square clips and adds captions.
- **Scheduling:** write captions, pick accounts and publish now or at a set
  time. Claude can list, edit, retry and delete posts, and show post analytics.

## How it connects

This plugin adds one remote MCP server, `https://clipcast.tryunsora.com/mcp`.
It runs no local code. The first time Claude uses it you sign in to your
ClipCast account with OAuth.

## Data it sends

Video links, uploaded files, captions and post settings you give Claude are
sent to ClipCast's servers to create clips and posts. Posts are published to
the social accounts you connected in ClipCast, only when you ask. Clipping
spends credits from your account, and posting needs a paid plan.
